Dataset Provenance: SMS Spam Collection Dataset

This model is trained on the benchmark SMS Spam Collection, a public corpus of 5,574 mobile text and message samples tagged as legitimate (Ham) or Spam. Please note: Because the dataset consists predominantly of short-form messages, learned vocabulary patterns reflect SMS communication styles. While the classifier performs with exceptional precision on concise phishing, promo, and urgency tactics, real enterprise emails may have additional structural characteristics (e.g. SPF/DKIM headers, attachments, HTML formatting) that require multi-layered security controls.
