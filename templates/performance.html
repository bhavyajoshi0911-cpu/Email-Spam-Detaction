Beginner-Friendly Metric Guide

How to interpret classification metrics in email security

Precision ({{ metrics.metrics.by_class.spam.precision }}% on Spam)

"When the model flags an email as Spam, how often is it actually spam?"
High precision is essential in spam detection because you do not want important client emails, bills, or password resets sent to the junk folder. A {{ metrics.metrics.by_class.spam.precision }}% precision means false alarms are extremely rare.

Recall ({{ metrics.metrics.by_class.spam.recall }}% on Spam)

"Out of all actual spam emails that arrived, how many did the filter catch?"
An 89.06% recall indicates that nearly 9 out of 10 spam messages are blocked, while a small fraction of borderline messages might slip through.

F1-Score ({{ metrics.metrics.by_class.spam.f1_score }}%)

The harmonic mean of precision and recall. It ensures neither precision nor recall was sacrificed to achieve high numbers on the other.

Why Accuracy Alone Can Be Misleading: Since ~87.5% of dataset messages are Ham, a naive dummy model that always guessed "Ham" would still achieve 87.5% accuracy while catching 0% of spam. That is why F1-Score and class-specific Precision/Recall provide the real picture.
